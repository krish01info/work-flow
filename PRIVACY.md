# Privacy Policy — reel builder

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

**reel builder** is a personal automation tool run by its owner (krish01info)
to publish short videos to the owner's own Instagram account. It is not
offered to the public and has no other users.

## What data the app accesses

- **Google Drive (`drive.file` scope only):** the app uploads video files it
  creates to the owner's own Google Drive so Instagram can fetch them, and
  deletes each file after it has been published. It cannot see or access any
  other file in the Drive.
- **Instagram / Meta:** the app publishes videos to the owner's own Instagram
  account and reads that account's post statistics (views, likes, comments,
  shares, saves).

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, store or process personal data of anyone other than
  the owner.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
- It does not use Google user data for advertising, and does not use it to
  train AI or machine-learning models.

## Storage and retention

Uploaded videos are kept in Google Drive only until Instagram has fetched them,
then deleted. Access tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets in
the owner's repository and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account
security settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
